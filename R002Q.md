## Q1 Locker Long-Deck Touch Scrolling
- **FAIL**

**Current behavior** (verified with a temporary 20-entry diagnostic run at 390×720; deck scroller measured `scrollHeight 1060` vs `clientHeight 566` — genuine overflow, `scrollTop` starts at 0):

1. **Upward drag on an inactive card:** the card lifts (`translateY(-100px)` observed mid-drag) and, at ≥48 px, **activates/docks** (`activated: true`). `scrollTop` never changes.
2. **Downward drag:** scrolls the deck **toward the top only**. From a pre-set `scrollTop` of 250, a 100 px downward drag moved it to 150 (verified). From `scrollTop` 0 it does nothing.
3. **Does scrollTop increase and decrease correctly?** No. It can only **decrease** under touch. The container itself can scroll (programmatic `scrollTop = 500` reached 494), but no gesture ever increases it.
4. **touchAction/pointer handling:** every inactive card sets `touchAction: 'none'` (computed style confirmed) and calls `setPointerCapture` on pointer-down. Cards tile the entire deck surface (64 px peek height, −12 px overlap), so there is no gap where native scrolling can occur. Native vertical scroll is fully suppressed.
5. **Can activation capture a gesture meant as scrolling?** Yes — *every* upward drag ≥48 px on a card is interpreted as card activation. There is no way to express "scroll down through the deck" at all.
6. **10–20+ cards reachable?** No. Entries render newest-first; with 20 entries, roughly the last 8–9 cards sit below the fold and are unreachable by one-finger touch. (The diagnostic's first probe accidentally targeted an off-screen card and every gesture was a no-op — direct evidence of unreachability.)
7. **Dead direction:** yes — increasing `scrollTop` (revealing later cards) is impossible via touch.
8. **Does the implementation distinguish scroll / activation-drag / tap?** Only partially: tap (<8 px) vs. drag (≥8 px) is distinguished, and drag *direction* picks lift (up) vs. scroll (down). There is no initial-direction "gesture claim" step and no way for an upward gesture to be a scroll.
9. **Root cause:** in `DeckInactiveCard.onPointerMove`, any upward delta is unconditionally routed to card lift (`setLift`) and downward delta to `scrollBy(lastY − clientY)`, which is always negative when moving down. Combined with `touchAction:'none'` + pointer capture on card surfaces that cover the whole deck, the scroller's native scrolling is unreachable and its `scrollTop` can only ever decrease.

**PASS/FAIL summary:**
- Long-deck touch scrolling upward through content (reaching later cards): **FAIL**
- Scrolling back downward (toward top): **PASS**
- Card activation still working (tap and upward drag on visible cards): **PASS**
- Normal vertical scroll not accidentally activating cards: **FAIL** (an intended upward scroll ≥48 px docks a card)

**Narrowest future correction (description only):** add an initial-direction disambiguation in the card gesture handler — on the first ~8–10 px of movement, if the gesture is downward *or* the deck overflows and the gesture is upward, hand the gesture to the deck scroller (both directions, `scrollBy` with the raw delta) and suppress lift; keep lift/dock only for upward gestures when the deck does not overflow, or gate docking on a firmer signal (e.g. tap always docks; drag-dock only from a non-overflowing deck). Isolated to `DeckInactiveCard`/`CardDeck` in `MobileFunctionalV3.tsx`.

## Q2 Locker Active-Title Synchronization
- **FAIL** (defect confirmed in source)

- **Current title state source:** local state `listName` (`useState('Untitled List')`), rendered in the centered title band (`{listName || 'Untitled List'}`). It is set on mount from saved metadata (or `'Demo Pack List'` for the demo seed) and by explicit rename paths only.
- **Load path behavior:** the Locker card's "Load This List" button calls `mutateSandbox(() => ({ items, order, meta }))`, then `closeDeck()` and a `Loaded "name"` toast. It never calls `setListName`.
- **Is the title updated?** No.
- **Why not:** the loaded `LockerEntry.store` carries only `items/order/meta`; the entry's `name` lives on the entry envelope and is used only for the toast and `aria-label`. No code path transfers it to `listName`.
- **Only display, or wider identity staleness?** Wider. `listName` also feeds **Save** (the saved-entry name is derived from `listName`) and share metadata (`name: listName`). After loading "Sample List", a subsequent Save would store the new content under the *old* title — active-list identity is stale, not just the display.
- **Narrowest future correction:** call `setListName(entry.name)` (optionally stripped of the auto-appended save timestamp suffix) inside the Load handler.
- **Safely isolated to MobileFunctionalV3.tsx?** Yes — one line in the Load button's onClick.
- **Risk to Locker storage / current list data:** none identified. Locker writes are append-only (`appendLockerEntry`); synchronizing the title touches only React state. The only behavioral side effect is the *intended* one (future saves/shares carry the correct name).

## Q3 Search Card Readability
- **FAIL** (defect confirmed in source)

- **Current styling behavior:** `DeckInactiveCard` applies `opacity: card.disabled ? 0.45 : 1` to the **entire card container** — the same element that carries the solid `background: CARD_BG` and border.
- **Root cause:** element-level `opacity` makes the card's background 55% transparent, so list/category content behind the deck bleeds through all three disabled Search cards (the backdrop is only rgba 0.45 black, so text behind remains legible enough to collide visually).
- **Text/icon vs. surface opacity coupled?** Yes — a single `opacity` declaration fades surface, border, text, and icon together.
- **Can the surface stay opaque with only content muted?** Yes — keep container `opacity: 1` with the solid background, and mute the *content* instead (muted text/icon colors, or opacity on the inner title/subtitle/icon spans only).
- **Narrowest future correction:** in `DeckInactiveCard`, replace the container-level `opacity` for disabled cards with muted foreground colors (or inner-element opacity), leaving `background: CARD_BG` fully opaque.
- **Effect on enabled cards:** none — the change is entirely inside the `card.disabled` branch.
- **Safely isolated:** yes — one component in `MobileFunctionalV3.tsx`; all four decks share it, so disabled cards elsewhere (e.g. "Create New List") also become readable.

## Q4 Safe Area
- **UNPROVEN** (and analytically at risk on nonzero insets)

- **Current measurements/offset logic** (measured in the diagnostic run, inset = 0):
  - Bottom nav: `position: sticky; minHeight: 58px; paddingBottom: calc(10px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))`; rendered `offsetHeight = 58px` at inset 0.
  - Card deck: `position: absolute; bottom: 58px` (`NAV_H` constant).
  - Backdrop: `position: absolute; bottom: 58px` (same constant).
  - The deck/backdrop offsets are **fixed constants**, *not* derived from the nav's actual rendered height.
- **Risk on nonzero inset:** the nav grows to ≈ `58 + inset` px (its padding includes the env inset), but the deck and backdrop stay anchored at `bottom: 58px`. On an iPhone-style device (inset ≈ 34 px), the bottom ~34 px of the deck — which is exactly where the **active card's action buttons** sit — slides *behind* the nav bar (nav zIndex 40 > deck zIndex 31). So yes: the deck can sit underneath the nav, and the nav can cover active-card controls.
- **Is the R002 "Safe Area PASS" claim proven by existing tests?** No. All Playwright runs execute with `safe-area-inset-bottom = 0`, where the constants coincidentally align. No test simulates a nonzero inset.
- **Exact future proof/test:** a Playwright test that (a) injects a nonzero inset — e.g. `page.addInitScript` installing a CSS override such as `@supports` shim or launching with `--use-mobile-user-agent` plus a stylesheet setting a custom property fallback (practically: override via `document.documentElement.style.setProperty` on a test build that reads a `--safe-area-bottom` var with `env()` fallback) — then (b) opens each deck, docks a card, and asserts `boundingBox(activeCardBottomButton).y + height <= boundingBox(navBar).y`. Note: truly emulating `env()` requires either the CSS-var indirection in app code or a real-device/DevTools sensor run; the report should state the chosen mechanism.
- **Narrowest future correction:** anchor the deck and backdrop with `bottom: calc(${NAV_H}px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))` — or measure the nav's rendered height — so all three surfaces share one source of truth.

## Q6 Closed-Deck Pointer Events
- **PASS**
- **Mount/unmount behavior:** decks are conditionally rendered (`{activeDeck === 'locker' && (<CardDeck …/>)}` etc.) — a closed deck and its backdrop are **completely unmounted**, not hidden. A closed deck cannot intercept anything because it has no DOM presence.
- **Transition behavior:** closing sets `activeDeck = null`, which unmounts synchronously on the next render — there is **no exit animation** and therefore no window where an invisible element lingers. (The rise animation exists only on open.)
- **Current test coverage:** explicitly asserted. "after closing a deck the list is fully interactive (no invisible overlay)" asserts `getByTestId('deck-backdrop')` **and** `getByRole('dialog')` both `toHaveCount(0)` after close, then clicks a category header and asserts it expands. Every close-path test also asserts `dialog … toHaveCount(0)`.
- **Recommended future assertion (optional hardening):** an elementFromPoint probe immediately after close — `page.evaluate(() => document.elementFromPoint(x, y))` at a list-item coordinate and at a tab-bar coordinate, asserting the hit element is the list/tab, not any overlay — executed within the same frame as the close click to guard against any future reintroduction of exit animations.

## Contradictions / Additional Material Findings
- The R002 report claimed "State preservation across deck open/close ✅" and "Safe-area insets ✅"; per Q5 and Q4 those claims exceed what the automation actually proves.
- The R002 24-test suite's drag tests only ever target **visible, near-top** cards in short decks, which is why the Q1 scrolling defect was never surfaced: no R002 test creates a deck taller than the viewport.
- The Locker deck refreshes entries on every open (`readLockerEntries()` on deck-open), so newly saved lists appear correctly — the Q1/Q2 issues are gesture/title issues, not data-freshness issues.
