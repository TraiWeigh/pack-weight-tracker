## Core classification rule

When an item is consumed, depleted, eaten, drunk, burned, applied, discarded, or used up during the trip, its destination must be:

Consumables

A known Consumables Type must override the original spreadsheet section.

The original section must not override a confirmed Consumables classification.

## Correct priority order

Determine Destination in this order:

1. Known Consumables Type
2. Explicit Consumables section or category
3. Original spreadsheet section
4. Default Type-to-category mapping
5. Manual review selection

This means a recognized consumable must move to Consumables even when it appears under another section such as Kitchen, Hygiene, First Aid, Water, Food, Electronics, or Dog Gear.
