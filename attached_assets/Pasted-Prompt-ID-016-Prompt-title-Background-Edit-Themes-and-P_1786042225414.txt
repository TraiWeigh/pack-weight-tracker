# PART 10 — DELETING A PERSONAL PHOTO

Custom photos may be deleted.

Built-in photos may not be deleted.

For a custom photo:

- Show a delete control on mouse hover.
- Also provide a keyboard-accessible delete control.
- Also provide a touch-accessible method that does not depend on hover.
- Require confirmation before deleting.

Confirmation must clearly identify that the photo will be removed from the personal background library.

Cancel:

- Leaves the photo unchanged.
- Leaves the current active background unchanged.

Confirm:

- Removes only that photo.
- Does not remove the collection.
- Does not remove other photos.
- Does not change unrelated saved checklist data.

If the deleted photo is currently active:

- Switch safely to the current default background or another documented fallback.
- Do not leave a broken image URL.
- Do not crash the Background panel.
- Record the fallback behavior in the report.
