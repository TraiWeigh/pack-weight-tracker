R001 — TrailWeigh Mobile Organic Drawer Diagnostic Audit
REPORT ID: R001
MODE: Economy recommended
SCOPE: DIAGNOSTIC ONLY — DO NOT MODIFY TRAILWEIGH APPLICATION CODE

PURPOSE
Inspect the current mobile implementation in artifacts/pack-checklist/src/pages/MobileFunctionalV3.tsx after the Three-Slider Redesign. Determine exactly how to convert the current visible/gutter-based sliders into the finalized organic mobile drawer system with the smallest safe implementation change.

FINALIZED TARGET BEHAVIOR TO AUDIT AGAINST
1. Side drawers overlay the list. They must not reserve permanent horizontal gutters or reduce category width.
2. The approximately 1/2-inch left/right invisible gesture regions may overlap category headers/wedges. They are not visible layout columns.
3. A normal category tap always remains a category tap.
4. Normal vertical movement remains list scrolling.
5. A side drawer captures only after deliberate horizontal movement crosses an intent threshold. When capture occurs, cancel the pending category tap.
6. Once captured, the drawer follows the thumb continuously and approximately 1:1, can reverse immediately without release, preserves release velocity, and settles with restrained spring/magnetic behavior.
7. Visible drawer icons remain tap/click alternatives and should not require hand repositioning.
8. Only one side drawer owns horizontal interaction at a time.
9. The bottom utility/Search panel is separate from the side-drawer gesture system:
   - tap the bottom bar to toggle;
   - touch/press on the bottom bar and drag vertically to move it;
   - dragging may reverse before release;
   - release position and velocity determine settling.
10. The bottom panel must not capture vertical gestures that begin outside the bottom bar.
11. Drawers/panels must preserve underlying category width, accordion state, scroll position, item state, and selection state.
12. Haptics must be restrained:
   - no continuous vibration;
   - use only subtle meaningful detent/snap feedback around capture/snap/open/closed events;
   - respect platform/OS capability and user settings;
   - do not fake unsupported haptics.
13. Reduced-motion and keyboard/accessibility alternatives must remain functional.
14. Controls that require their own horizontal interaction, text editing/selection, sliders, reorder interactions, or similar behavior must not have their gestures stolen by a global drawer recognizer.
