2. CATEGORY REORDER — TAP, HOLD, MOVE, RELEASE
============================================================

Replace the six-dot reorder initiation with direct category-bar long-press reordering.

INTERACTION MODEL

A. QUICK TAP
- A normal quick tap on the category retains the existing category open/close accordion behavior.
- A quick tap must NOT start reorder.

B. TAP + HOLD
- Press and hold directly on the category bar to enter reorder mode.
- Use a native-feeling long-press threshold appropriate for mobile (roughly 350–450 ms is acceptable; choose one stable value and document it).
- Do not require a visible reorder handle.
- Once the hold threshold is reached, the category visibly rises/floats and reorder mode becomes active.
- If appropriate and genuinely supported, one subtle haptic may acknowledge entry into reorder mode; do not fake unsupported haptics.

C. BEFORE HOLD ACTIVATES
- If the user begins a meaningful vertical scroll before the long-press threshold, normal list scrolling wins and reorder must not activate.
- If the user begins the approved right-edge-to-left delete swipe before long-press activation, slide-to-delete wins and reorder must not activate.
- A small amount of finger jitter must not accidentally cancel a legitimate hold, but meaningful movement should disambiguate toward scroll/swipe.

D. WHILE REORDERING
- The floating category follows the finger directly through the full vertical drag distance.
- Stable category identity must be used so the floating style never jumps to the wrong category.
- The category remains visually above the stack.
- Preserve its wedge, color, text, counts, and weight.
- The valid reorder target dims slightly.
- Only one valid target dims at a time.
- Previous target undims immediately when the dragged category moves away.
- The user may reverse drag direction naturally.

E. RELEASE
- Releasing the finger commits the category into the indicated new position.
- The category settles smoothly and FLUSH into the category stack.
- All dimming/elevation/temporary transforms clear.
- If the long press activates but the category is released without crossing a valid reorder boundary, keep it in its original position and settle cleanly.

F. CANCEL / INTERRUPTION
Cancel the drag safely without committing if:
- pointer/touch is cancelled;
- app/window loses focus;
- document becomes hidden;
- another gesture legitimately takes over.

No category may remain floating/stuck.

3. CATEGORY REORDER MOTION MUST BE SMOOTHER
============================================================

The category bars that move out of the way during reorder need smoother motion.

REQUIRED RESULT
- The dragged category continues to track the finger directly 1:1.
- OTHER category bars should animate smoothly into their temporary/reordered positions as space opens.
- No abrupt jumping.
- No visible jitter.
- No flicker.
- No repeated snap-back/snap-forward while crossing a boundary.
- No slow or exaggerated animation.
- No springy bounce/overshoot.

TARGET DIMMING
- Target dimming should transition smoothly but quickly.
- When the target changes, the old target should return to normal immediately/smoothly and the new target should become subtly dimmed.
- No multiple dim targets.

RELEASE
- Final settle into the flush stack should be quick and smooth.
- Motion should feel native and controlled.

REDUCED MOTION
- Respect reduced-motion settings by minimizing nonessential transition animation while preserving clear state change.
