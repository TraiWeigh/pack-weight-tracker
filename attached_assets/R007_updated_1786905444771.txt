============================================================
1. TERMINOLOGY — USE THIS EXACTLY
============================================================

BOX
An individual control in the bottom control bar.

BOX GROUP
A related group of bottom-bar control boxes.

STACKED BAR
A compact deck/content bar above the bottom controls.
A stacked bar is NOT a control box.

IMPORTANT SEPARATION RULE
A control box must NEVER appear:
- inside a stacked bar;
- on top of a stacked bar;
- attached to a stacked bar;
- embedded in a stacked bar.

Control boxes belong ONLY to the bottom box-group system.

============================================================
2. STACKED BARS — MATCH CATEGORY SIZE, NEVER REORDER
============================================================

REQUIRED
- Stacked bars use the same height and overall compact proportion as category bars.
- Keep square/flush TrailWeigh structural styling.
- Keep appropriate internal padding.
- Do not make stacked bars large Wallet/credit-card-style cards.

CRITICAL INTERACTION RULE
Stacked bars MUST NOT be movable/reorderable like categories.

Specifically:
- category long-press reorder must not activate on stacked bars;
- stacked bars must not rise/follow the finger;
- stacked bars must not change order from a long press or drag;
- a normal vertical gesture over a scrollable stacked-bar list remains scrolling;
- tapping a stacked bar performs its normal action only.

Do not add a visible drag/reorder handle to stacked bars.

============================================================
5. BOX GROUPS CAN MOVE BOTH DIRECTIONS
============================================================

The box-group system is bidirectional.

Exact sequence:

1. GROUP 1 slides LEFT  -> GROUP 2
2. GROUP 2 slides LEFT  -> GROUP 3
3. GROUP 3 slides LEFT  -> GROUP 4

Reverse:

4. GROUP 4 slides RIGHT -> GROUP 3
5. GROUP 3 slides RIGHT -> GROUP 2
6. GROUP 2 slides RIGHT -> GROUP 1

NO WRAPAROUND
- Group 4 does not continue left to Group 1.
- Group 1 does not continue right to Group 4.

The bar behaves as one linear horizontal sequence:

GROUP 1 <-> GROUP 2 <-> GROUP 3 <-> GROUP 4

============================================================
6. SWIPE + STATIONARY CHEVRON CONTROL
============================================================

Every group transition must be possible in TWO equivalent ways:

A. gesture:
- user physically swipes/slides the bottom bar left or right;

B. stationary/tappable control:
- user taps a directional CHEVRON BOX.

Both methods MUST produce the same transition.

CHEVRON MEANING

RIGHT-SIDE NEXT CHEVRON
- physically slides the current box group LEFT
- reveals the next numbered group

LEFT-SIDE BACK CHEVRON
- physically slides the current box group RIGHT
- reveals the previous numbered group

CHEVRONS
- match the size/style of other bottom control boxes;
- have accessible names such as "Previous controls" and "Next controls";
- must remain visually obvious as stationary alternatives to the swipe gesture.

Do not rely on swipe alone.

- normal vertical list scroll.

============================================================
8. BOX-GROUP COMPLETION HAPTIC
============================================================

Add one restrained haptic confirmation when a box group COMPLETES a real move
and settles fully into a different group position.

REQUIRED
- NO haptic while the group is physically sliding.
- ONE light haptic only when the new group finishes settling.
- Same completion haptic whether the move was started by swipe or chevron.
- If a swipe is cancelled/too short and the group returns to its starting position:
  NO haptic.
- Do not fire one haptic for the chevron tap and another for settle.
- Exactly one completion haptic per successful group change.
- Respect device/browser/system support and settings.
- Do not fake haptics on unsupported platforms.

============================================================
9. CATEGORY REORDER COMPLETION HAPTIC
============================================================

Use the same interaction language for category reorder.

REQUIRED
- NO haptic while dragging a category.
- NO repeated haptics while crossing category boundaries.
- If the category is released and successfully moves to a NEW position:
  fire ONE light haptic when it settles into place.
- If the category is released and returns to/or remains in its original position:
  NO haptic.
- Do not change the existing R006 long-press reorder mechanics except for this
  completion haptic and any necessary nonfunctional wiring.
- Respect device/browser/system support and settings.
- Do not fake haptics on unsupported platforms.

Interaction principle:
move -> settle -> one subtle confirmation
