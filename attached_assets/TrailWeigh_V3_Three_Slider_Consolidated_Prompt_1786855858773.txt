TRAILWEIGH — V3 PHONE EDGE-SLIDER REDESIGN — CONSOLIDATED CURRENT REQUIREMENTS

STRICT SCOPE
- V3 phone experience only.
- Preserve all currently passing functionality.
- Do not change Checklist.tsx.
- Do not change desktop.
- Do not change database, auth, deployment/publishing, or unrelated behavior.
- Do not redesign the contents of the existing panels unless explicitly stated below.
- If any requirement conflicts with existing architecture, STOP and report the conflict rather than broadening scope.

1. TOP / TITLE STRUCTURE
- Keep the TrailWeigh logo header as its own separate top header.
- Directly below it, keep the actual active list title centered horizontally in its own title band.
- The left hamburger cap and right + cap belong on opposite sides of this centered title space.
- Neither cap/bar may touch or overlap the TrailWeigh logo header above.
- Neither cap/bar may touch or overlap the Summary bar below.
- The list title must remain visually centered even with the left and right controls present.

2. LEFT EDGE CONTROL — NAVIGATION
- Replace the small pull tab with a larger thumb-friendly vertical slide bar.
- Use a subdued, non-intrusive TrailWeigh-compatible muted slate-green / gray-green color, not red.
- The visible bar may stay visually slim, but its touch/grab target must be comfortably thumb-sized.
- The hamburger icon is physically attached to the TOP of the left slide bar as one continuous piece.
- Hamburger cap and slide bar use the same color; hamburger icon uses high-contrast light/white treatment.
- The hamburger cap sits beside the centered list-title band, not in the logo header.
- The left slide bar must not overlap or touch category cards or expanded category/item content.
- Reduce the list/category content width slightly if necessary to create a deliberate left-side gutter.
- Dragging the left bar horizontally must move the navigation drawer 1:1 with the user's finger, with no lag or fixed-duration animation while dragging.
- When fully open, the drawer should travel almost completely across the phone.
- Leave the left slide bar exposed at the drawer edge so the user can grab the SAME bar and drag the drawer closed.
- On release, smoothly settle fully open or closed based on drag distance/velocity.
- No bounce, wobble, or overshoot.
- Preserve tap activation, keyboard activation, Escape close, backdrop close, focus handling, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do not add a separate floating hamburger control.

3. RIGHT EDGE CONTROL — ADD / CREATE
- Add a matching thumb-friendly vertical slide bar on the right side.
- Same subdued muted slate-green / gray-green family as the left bar.
- The + icon is physically attached to the TOP of the right slide bar as one continuous piece.
- + cap and slide bar use the same color; + icon uses high-contrast light/white treatment.
- The + cap sits beside the centered list-title band, not in the logo header.
- The right slide bar must not overlap or touch category cards or expanded category/item content.
- Reduce the list/category content width slightly if necessary to create a deliberate right-side gutter.
- The right bar controls the existing Add/Create panel.
- Preserve the existing Add/Create panel contents, labels, enabled/disabled states, and actions exactly.
- Dragging the right bar horizontally must move its panel 1:1 with the user's finger.
- The bar must remain exposed in the open state so it can be grabbed and slid back closed.
- Tapping the + cap should also open the Add/Create panel.
- On release, smoothly settle fully open or closed based on drag distance/velocity.
- No bounce, wobble, or overshoot.
- Preserve accessibility and reduced-motion behavior.
- Remove any redundant floating + control once this attached + cap fully replaces it.

4. CATEGORY / CONTENT WIDTH
- The category area must never sit underneath either side slide bar.
- Provide intentional left and right gutters if needed.
- Category cards, category text, checkboxes, controls, expanded items, and item details must remain fully readable and tappable.
- Test narrow widths carefully so the side controls do not make category content unusably narrow.

5. BOTTOM BAR
- KEEP THE EXISTING BOTTOM BAR VISUALLY AND FUNCTIONALLY AS IT IS.
- Do not replace it.
- Do not redesign its existing icons, labels, contents, height, or current functions.
- Add a thumb-friendly horizontal slide HANDLE physically attached to the TOP EDGE of the existing bottom bar.
- The handle and bottom bar should read as one connected component.
- Use the same subdued muted slate-green / gray-green color family as the side sliders.
- The visible handle should be restrained, but its touch target must be comfortable for a thumb.
- Dragging the attached handle upward/downward must control the existing More/task panel 1:1 with the user's finger.
- The handle must remain exposed and usable so the panel can be slid back closed.
- Tapping the handle should also open the existing More/task panel.
- On release, smoothly settle fully open or closed based on drag distance/velocity.
- No bounce, wobble, or overshoot.
- Preserve the existing More/task panel contents and actions exactly.

6. SCROLLING
- Preserve normal vertical scrolling.
- Do not require a permanently visible right-side scrollbar.
- A normal temporary OS/browser scroll indicator while actively scrolling is acceptable.

7. MOTION STANDARD
- While the user is actively dragging any slider, movement must be direct 1:1 finger tracking.
- No easing or lag during the drag itself.
- After release, use a quick, smooth native-feeling settle animation.
- Opening may decelerate smoothly; closing may be slightly faster.
- No spring bounce or overshoot.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion / reduced-motion settings.

8. ACCESSIBILITY
- All icon caps and slider handles must have meaningful accessible labels.
- Tap/click must remain available; dragging cannot be the only activation method.
- Keyboard operation must remain available where applicable.
- Preserve focus management and Escape/backdrop close behavior for overlays/panels.
- Touch target size should be thumb-friendly even if the visible bar is slimmer.

9. RESPONSIVE VERIFICATION
Test at:
- 320 px
- 375 px
- 390 px
- 430 px

Verify at every width:
- TrailWeigh logo header is untouched by the sliders.
- Actual list title remains centered.
- Hamburger cap and + cap sit on opposite sides of the title band.
- Neither side control touches the logo header or Summary bar.
- Side bars do not overlap categories/items.
- Category width remains usable.
- Left drawer opens/closes with 1:1 drag and leaves its bar exposed.
- Right Add/Create panel opens/closes with 1:1 drag and leaves its bar exposed.
- Bottom existing bar remains unchanged except for its attached top slide handle.
- Bottom handle opens/closes the More/task panel with 1:1 drag.
- Tapping each control also works.
- No horizontal overflow.
- No permanent right scrollbar is introduced.
