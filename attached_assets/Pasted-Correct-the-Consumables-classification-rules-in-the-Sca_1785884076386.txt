Correct the Consumables classification rules in the Scan Gear List importer.

Do not modify the working upload pipeline, spreadsheet column detection, review screen, or existing Type, Description, and Weight extraction.

## Core rule

For this application, an item is a Consumable when its usable contents are consumed, depleted, applied, eaten, drunk, burned, discarded, or used up during the trip.

Classify the complete listed item as Consumables even when its listed weight includes disposable packaging or a container.

Do not attempt to subtract container or packaging weight.

Examples:

- A full fuel canister is Consumables.
- A toothpaste tube containing toothpaste is Consumables.
- Sunscreen in its original bottle is Consumables.
- Food packaged in a freezer bag is Consumables.
- Water carried in a disposable bottle is Consumables.
- Medication in its original container is Consumables.
- Dog food in its packaging is Consumables.

## Reusable-container rule

A reusable container is durable gear only when the user lists it as a separate item or row.

Examples:

Row 1:
Type: Toothpaste
Description: Travel Toothpaste Tube
Weight: 0.8 oz
Destination: Consumables

Row 2:
Type: Toiletry Bottle
Description: Reusable Silicone Bottle
Weight: 0.4 oz
Destination: Hygiene

Row 1:
Type: Fuel
Description: 4 oz Isobutane Canister
Weight: 7.68 oz
Destination: Consumables

Row 2:
Type: Fuel Bottle
Description: Refillable Alcohol Fuel Bottle
Weight: 1.1 oz
Destination: Kitchen

Do not split one imported row into separate contents and container records.

Do not create an estimated empty-container weight.

Do not ask the importer to determine how much of the listed weight is contents versus packaging.

Preserve the source row exactly as one Type, one Description, and one Weight.

## Classification priority

Choose the destination in this order:

1. Explicit recognized section heading from the uploaded list
2. Explicit destination or category column
3. Known Consumables Type
4. Manual user selection on the review screen

When a recognized Consumables Type appears under another explicit source section, preserve the source organization unless the source section itself is generic or missing.

Example:

Section: Hygiene
Type: Toothpaste

The importer may keep Destination as Hygiene because that is how the uploaded list is organized.

Example with no section:

Type: Toothpaste

The importer should use Destination: Consumables.

## Preserve specific Type

Do not replace specific Types with the generic word Consumables.

Correct:

Destination: Consumables
Type: Sunscreen
Description: Blue Lizard Mineral Sunscreen
Weight: 2 oz

Incorrect:

Destination: Consumables
Type: Consumables
Description: Blue Lizard Mineral Sunscreen
Weight: 2 oz

Only retain Type as Consumables when the source provides no more specific recognizable Type.

Do not combine neighboring rows.

Do not estimate consumed quantity.

Do not subtract packaging weight.

Do not change the supplied Weight.
