# Prompt 021P — Align Pack Summary Top Edge With Checklist Panel

## Goal

Correct the remaining desktop vertical alignment issue shown in the user's latest screenshot.

The **top edge of the Pack Summary panel on the right must line up exactly with the top edge of the checklist/category panel on the left**.

Currently:

* Left checklist/category panel begins at approximately **Y = 104 px**
* Pack Summary begins at approximately **Y = 120 px**

The Pack Summary is therefore approximately **16 px too low**.

Move the Pack Summary/right sidebar content upward approximately **16 px** so the two content panels share the same top edge.

---

---

## Diagnose the Cause Before Changing It

The Prompt 021N structure places the left and right content areas in the same content grid:

```text
Content Area
→ Left checklist/category scroll column
→ Right sidebar scroll column
```

Because both columns already share the same parent grid, their top edges should naturally align.

Inspect the right sidebar and Pack Summary markup/CSS for an extra approximately **16 px vertical offset**, such as:

* `pt-4`
* `mt-4`
* top padding
* top margin
* spacer element
* unnecessary wrapper spacing

Also compare it directly with the first checklist/category panel on the left.

### Preferred Fix

Remove or correct the **actual extra spacing causing the Pack Summary to sit 16 px lower**.

Do not hide the problem with a positioning hack.

Avoid using:

* negative margin
* `translateY`
* `top: -16px`
* absolute positioning
* transforms

unless there is an existing architectural reason that makes one of those necessary.

The preferred result is that both content columns begin naturally at the same vertical position because their spacing rules match.
