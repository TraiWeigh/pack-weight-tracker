MEANING OF THE FIELDS
Use this meaning consistently:

TYPE
= what kind of item it is.
Examples:
- Tent
- Backpack
- Quilt
- Hiking Shirt
- Power Bank
- Water Filter

NAME
= the actual manufacturer/product/model name shown to the user.
Examples:
- Zpacks Duplex
- Durston Kakwa 55
- Enlightened Equipment Enigma 20°
- Outdoor Research Echo
- Nitecore NB10000
- Sawyer Squeeze

WEIGHT
= per-item weight.

QTY
= quantity.

TOTAL
= calculated total weight.

IMPORTANT:
Worn, consumable, carried, status, and routing text are NOT item names.

- "Worn while hiking"
- "Worn item"
- "Pair weight"
- "Consumable"
- "Carried layer"
- "Reusable"
- "Three daily portions"
- "quantity > 1"
- "generic category"

Those values may remain useful as internal metadata/routing clues or notes if the current architecture supports them, but they must not be treated as the product Name.
