SAFE HEURISTIC GUIDANCE

Do NOT implement a naive “two words = product name” rule.

Prefer conservative signals such as:
- two or more product-name-like tokens;
- brand/model-style capitalization;
- uppercase/alphanumeric model tokens such as NB10000, XLite, X-Mid, Mini 2;
- a proper-name-like manufacturer token followed by a model/product token.

Reject obvious patterns such as:
- unit/capacity/spec-only text: mAh, ml, L, oz, g, kg, degree, °, inches, cm, mm, etc.;
- status/use wording: worn, carried, reusable, consumable, rechargeable;
- quantity/weight notes;
- generic descriptive phrases where the words are common product characteristics rather than brand/model identity.

IMPORTANT:
If uncertain, leave NAME blank.

False negatives are safer than false positives here.
