# Prompt 022 — Add TrailWeigh Footer, Legal, Help & Support Navigation

## Goal

Add a clean, compact footer to TrailWeigh that gives users easy access to:

* information about TrailWeigh
* Help & How-To
* support
* privacy information
* legal information
* account/data information

The footer should be appropriately sized for TrailWeigh.

Do **not** create a large Amazon-style directory footer.

The footer should remain visually simple and contain approximately **8–10 useful links**.

---

# 1. Footer Structure

Create three compact footer sections:

## TrailWeigh

Include:

* About TrailWeigh
* How It Works

## Help

Include:

* Help & How-To
* Report a Problem
* Contact Us

## Account & Privacy

Include:

* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Use
* Delete Account / Data
* Affiliate Disclosure
* Accessibility

At the bottom of the footer display:

**© 2026 TrailWeigh · All rights reserved.**

---

# 2. Help & How-To

Use the wording:

**Help & How-To**

Do not call this simply “FAQ.”

This page will eventually contain:

* Getting Started
* How-To Videos
* Step-by-Step Guides
* Frequently Asked Questions
* Troubleshooting

The actual how-to videos do **not** need to be created in this prompt.

Create the page/navigation structure so videos and guides can be added later.

---

# 3. How It Works

Keep **How It Works** separate from **Help & How-To**.

The distinction is:

**How It Works**
= an overview of what TrailWeigh does and how the overall workflow works.

**Help & How-To**
= detailed instructions showing users exactly how to perform individual tasks.

Do not combine these two pages.

---

# 4. Report a Problem

Make **Report a Problem** easy to find.

It should appear directly in the Help section of the footer.

For now, connect it to the existing problem-reporting location if one already exists.

If a dedicated Report a Problem page or route is needed, create the route/page shell.

Do **not** build the screen-recording system in this prompt.

The built-in problem-reporting recorder will be handled in a later prompt.

---

# 5. Contact Us

Create a Contact Us destination.

The page should be structured so TrailWeigh can later use either:

* an official TrailWeigh support email address
* or a contact form

Do **not** expose a developer's or owner's personal email address.

If no official TrailWeigh support email has been configured yet, do not invent one.

Use neutral temporary wording such as:

**TrailWeigh support contact information will be available here.**

Do not create fake contact information.

---

# 6. Privacy Policy

Add a working:

**Privacy Policy**

link and page/route.

For this prompt, create the page structure only.

The complete Privacy Policy will be written and reviewed separately.

The eventual policy will need to address topics such as:

* account information
* saved gear lists
* uploaded/imported information
* cookies/local browser storage
* analytics, if used
* service providers
* data retention
* account deletion
* user privacy rights
* how user information is protected

Do not invent legal promises or privacy practices that have not been verified.

---

# 7. Terms of Use

Add a working:

**Terms of Use**

link and page/route.

For this prompt, create the page structure only.

The complete Terms of Use will be written and reviewed separately.

Do not generate extensive legal terms or make unverified legal claims in this prompt.

---

# 8. Delete Account / Data

Add:

**Delete Account / Data**

to the Account & Privacy section.

If TrailWeigh already has an account deletion/data deletion screen, route the footer link to that existing feature.

Do not duplicate an existing deletion system.

If the deletion feature does not yet exist, create only the appropriate page/route/navigation destination.

Do **not** implement a new destructive account-deletion backend as part of this prompt.

Do not change existing account-deletion behavior.

---

# 9. Affiliate Disclosure

Add:

**Affiliate Disclosure**

to the footer.

Create a simple disclosure page.

For now, it may contain wording substantially similar to:

**TrailWeigh may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through retailer links at no additional cost to you.**

Also make clear that the wording may need to be updated later to match the actual affiliate programs and retailer agreements TrailWeigh joins.

Do not claim TrailWeigh currently participates in any specific affiliate program unless that is already verified in the application.

Do not add retailer names that have not been confirmed.

---

# 10. Accessibility

Add:

**Accessibility**

to the footer.

Create a basic accessibility-information page/route.

The page should explain that TrailWeigh intends to make its application usable by people with disabilities and provide a future location for:

* accessibility information
* keyboard-navigation information
* screen-reader information
* contrast/readability information
* reporting an accessibility problem

Do not claim formal accessibility certification or full compliance with a specific standard unless it has actually been verified.

---

# 11. Sign-Up / Account Creation Links

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use must **not exist only in the footer**.

On the TrailWeigh account-creation/sign-up screen, add visible links to:

* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Use

Use concise wording such as:

**By creating an account, you agree to TrailWeigh's Terms of Use and acknowledge the Privacy Policy.**

Make:

**Terms of Use**

and:

**Privacy Policy**

clickable links to the same pages used by the footer.

Do not create duplicate policy pages.

---

# 12. Footer Appearance

The footer must have a **constant dark appearance**, regardless of whether TrailWeigh is in Light mode or Dark mode.

Do not make the footer change between light and dark themes.

Use:

* a **very dark charcoal background**
* white or very light-gray section headings
* white or light-gray links
* slightly muted light-gray copyright text

The footer should be visually similar in concept to a traditional dark website footer, but much smaller and cleaner than Amazon's footer.

Do **not** use pure black unless that better matches TrailWeigh's existing design system.

The footer should remain dark in:

* Light mode
* Dark mode
* System mode

---

# 13. Footer Font Sizes

Keep footer typography slightly smaller than the main TrailWeigh interface.

Use approximately:

### Section headings

**14 px**

Use semibold or equivalent existing TrailWeigh font weight.

### Footer links

**14 px**

Do not make links smaller than this simply to reduce footer height.

### Copyright / legal line

Use approximately:

**12–13 px**

### Link line height

Use approximately:

**20–22 px**

This should keep the footer compact while still being easy to read.

Do **not** use 10 px or 11 px text for normal footer links.

The footer should look visually smaller than the main application without becoming difficult to read.

---

# 14. Footer Size

Keep the footer intentionally compact.

Do not:

* create dozens of links
* add large marketing blocks
* add newsletter signup
* add social-media sections
* add retailer logos
* add app-store badges
* add promotional banners
* add large graphics
* add unnecessary navigation categories

The current footer should contain only the links specified in this prompt.

---

# 15. Desktop Layout

On desktop, use a compact multi-column layout.

The three footer groups should normally appear side-by-side when sufficient width is available.

Keep:

* reasonable spacing between columns
* modest vertical padding
* compact link spacing

Do not make the footer unusually tall.

---

# 16. Mobile Layout

On mobile:

* stack the three footer groups cleanly
* keep all links easy to tap
* preserve the same dark-charcoal background
* preserve readable 14 px link text
* do not introduce horizontal scrolling

Do not shrink the text excessively on mobile.

---

# 17. Placement

Place the footer at the bottom of the appropriate TrailWeigh application pages.

It should not interfere with:

* checklist scrolling
* Pack Summary
* toolbar positioning
* sidebar scrolling
* preview
* shared-list view
* Locker
* modal dialogs

Inspect TrailWeigh's current application layout before choosing the footer implementation.

Do not simply place the footer inside a scroll container if doing so would break the existing checklist/sidebar layout.

Use the existing application architecture appropriately.

---

# 18. Shared-Link Viewer

Review whether the footer should appear in the shared-link viewer.

If normal site navigation/footer elements already appear there, keep the experience consistent.

However, do not expose owner-only account controls to a person viewing somebody else's shared list.

In particular, a shared viewer must not gain access to another user's:

* account settings
* Delete Account / Data controls
* private Locker controls
* private saved files

Public informational links such as:

* About TrailWeigh
* How It Works
* Help & How-To
* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Use
* Affiliate Disclosure
* Accessibility

may remain accessible.

---

# 19. Existing Layout Protection

Do not change any previously approved Prompt 021 layout work.

Preserve:

* toolbar group positioning
* toolbar top spacing
* checklist/Pack Summary top-edge alignment
* desktop gutters
* sidebar width
* toolbar grouping
* filename positioning
* Hide behavior
* Preview behavior
* Imperial/Metric behavior
* Background Edit behavior
* Share behavior

Prompt 021P is visually approved.

Do not use this footer work as an opportunity to modify the toolbar or checklist geometry.

---

# 20. Navigation Requirements

Every footer link must:

* be keyboard accessible
* have a clear visible label
* use the correct route
* not lead to a 404
* remain readable against the dark footer
* work in Light mode
* work in Dark mode
* work on desktop
* work on mobile

Avoid unnecessary new-tab behavior for internal TrailWeigh pages.

Internal TrailWeigh informational pages should normally open within the application/site.

---

# 21. Page Heading Requirements

Each destination page should have a clear heading corresponding to its footer link.

Examples:

* About TrailWeigh
* How It Works
* Help & How-To
* Report a Problem
* Contact Us
* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Use
* Delete Account / Data
* Affiliate Disclosure
* Accessibility

Use the existing TrailWeigh page styling.

Do not over-design these pages.

---

# 22. Do Not Write Final Legal Documents Yet

This is important.

Do not generate large, supposedly final:

* Privacy Policies
* Terms of Use
* legal disclaimers
* accessibility compliance statements

without knowing TrailWeigh's final data practices and services.

This prompt establishes:

**navigation + page structure + basic safe placeholder/informational content.**

The final legal text will be handled separately.

# 24. Add Regression Tests

Add appropriate automated tests protecting:

* required footer links
* correct footer routes
* Privacy Policy link on sign-up
* Terms of Use link on sign-up
* shared-view protection against owner-only controls
* constant dark footer appearance
* footer responsiveness where practical

Do not write tests that only search arbitrary source-code text if a functional test can better protect the behavior.

* preserve user accounts
