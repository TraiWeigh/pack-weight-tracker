8. ADD ITEM BAR OVERFLOW CHEVRON — REPAIR
============================================================

The right-side chevron belongs to the contextual Add Item bar.

It must remain visible whenever the active long category is in bounded overflow mode.

State rules:

A. Hidden content BELOW:
- chevron points DOWN

B. At the bottom, hidden content exists ABOVE and none below:
- chevron points UP

C. Both above and below are possible:
- use ONE chevron only
- while later content remains below, point DOWN
- once the bottom is reached, change to UP

D. No overflow:
- chevron hidden or clearly disabled

Tap:
- DOWN -> move approximately one visible-page increment toward later items
- UP -> move approximately one visible-page increment toward earlier items
- smooth settle
- no spring/bounce
- do not jump end-to-end unless one page naturally reaches the end

Manual scrolling:
- still works
- chevron direction updates from actual current scroll position

Accessibility:
- DOWN = "Show later items"
- UP = "Show earlier items"
