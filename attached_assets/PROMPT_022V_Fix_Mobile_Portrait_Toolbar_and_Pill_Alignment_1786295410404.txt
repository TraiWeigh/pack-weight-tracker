1. DESIGN GOAL
==================================================

TrailWeigh must be fully usable in PHONE PORTRAIT orientation.

Landscape may remain supported, but users must NOT be forced to rotate the
phone simply to reach or understand toolbar controls.

Primary target:

iPhone portrait and similar-width Android phones.

==================================================
2. STANDARD UI APPROACH
==================================================

Use responsive grouping/wrapping.

Do NOT try to squeeze every control into one desktop-width row.

Preferred behavior on narrow screens:

- Keep related controls grouped.
- Allow groups to wrap onto additional rows.
- Preserve logical left-to-right order within each group.
- Keep consistent spacing.
- Keep controls centered or evenly aligned within available width.
- Avoid off-screen controls.
- Avoid overlapping pills.
- Avoid text clipping.

This is a RESPONSIVE TOOLBAR / CONTROL GROUP problem.
