1. FINAL TERMINOLOGY

BOX
One individual control in the bottom control bar.

BOX GROUP
A related collection of bottom control boxes.

STACKED BAR
A compact deck/content bar displayed above the bottom control system.

CATEGORY BAR
A main list category row/bar.

IMPORTANT SEPARATION RULE
A CONTROL BOX MUST NEVER APPEAR:
- inside a stacked bar;
- on a stacked bar;
- attached to a stacked bar;
- floating over a stacked bar;
- embedded as part of a stacked bar.

Control boxes belong ONLY to the bottom box-group system.

NO DUPLICATE CONTROL RULE

When a function is assigned a bottom control BOX, that bottom box becomes
the ONE mobile V3 control entry point for that function.

This means:
- MOVE the function into its approved bottom box;
- REMOVE any separate duplicate control for that same function from stacked bars,
  decks, headers, or other mobile V3 control areas;
- do NOT leave two controls that perform the same action merely because one existed before.

Examples include:
- Undo
- Redo
- Reset
- Preview
- Share
- Search
- Locker
- Summary
- Add
- Camera
- Photos
- More

IMPORTANT:
The original control does NOT have to already be in a box.
If Undo/Redo/Preview/etc. currently live in a stacked bar, relocate them into
their approved bottom boxes and remove the old copies.

This rule applies to the CONTROL itself, not to the content it opens.
For example:
- Preview may open a Preview panel;
- More may open More content;
- Summary may open Summary content.

Do not remove legitimate content merely because its launcher moved.

5. BOX GROUPS SLIDE BOTH DIRECTIONS

The user can move the box groups left OR right.

GROUP 1 <-> GROUP 2 <-> GROUP 3 <-> GROUP 4

EXACT PHYSICAL DIRECTION

FORWARD:
1. Group 1 slides LEFT -> Group 2
2. Group 2 slides LEFT -> Group 3
3. Group 3 slides LEFT -> Group 4

BACKWARD:
4. Group 4 slides RIGHT -> Group 3
5. Group 3 slides RIGHT -> Group 2
6. Group 2 slides RIGHT -> Group 1

NO WRAPAROUND
- Group 4 must NOT slide left and wrap to Group 1.
- Group 1 must NOT slide right and wrap to Group 4.

6. SWIPE + STATIONARY CHEVRON CONTROL

Every group movement must have TWO equivalent controls:

A. SWIPE / THUMB SLIDE
The user physically drags the bottom bar left or right.

B. CHEVRON BOX
A stationary/tappable alternative for users who do not want to swipe.

Both methods MUST perform the same group transition.

RIGHT-SIDE NEXT CHEVRON
- located on the RIGHT edge of Groups 1, 2, and 3;
- tapping it physically moves the current group LEFT;
- reveals the next numbered group.

LEFT-SIDE BACK CHEVRON
- located on the LEFT edge of Groups 2, 3, and 4;
- tapping it physically moves the current group RIGHT;
- reveals the previous numbered group.

CHEVRON REQUIREMENTS
- chevron is a control BOX;
- match the height/width system of other bottom boxes;
- provide accessible labels such as "Previous controls" and "Next controls";
- do not depend on swipe discovery alone.

- no springy/bouncy animation;
- no exaggerated overshoot;
- no horizontal page overflow;
- respect safe-area bottom inset;
- changing groups does NOT automatically open Locker/Summary/Add/etc.;
- active list remains visible while only the bottom controls change.

Do not break:
- category long-press reorder;
- category/item slide-to-delete;
- normal vertical scrolling.

8. BOX-GROUP HAPTIC — FINAL RULE

Provide ONE subtle/light haptic when a box group has COMPLETELY moved
and settles successfully into a DIFFERENT group position.

REQUIRED
- NO haptic while the group is moving;
- NO repeated haptics during the drag;
- exactly ONE light haptic after a successful completed group change;
- same completion haptic whether initiated by swipe or chevron;
- if movement is cancelled/too short and the group returns to its original position: NO haptic;
- do not produce one haptic for chevron tap plus another on settle;
- completion/settle gets the single confirmation;
- respect device/browser/system haptic support/settings;
- do not fake haptics where unsupported.
