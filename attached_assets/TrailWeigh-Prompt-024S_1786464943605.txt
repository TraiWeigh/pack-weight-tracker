UNKNOWN-ITEM SAFETY RULE

Do NOT guess when the item cannot be confidently classified.

If:
- source group is vague; AND
- the item is genuinely unknown to TrailWeigh's existing item recognition;

then preserve enough source organization so the item is not lost.

Acceptable behavior:
- keep the original source group for the unknown item; OR
- use an existing safe fallback category if TrailWeigh already has one.

Do NOT:
- discard the item;
- invent a highly specific category without evidence;
- force every vague-group item into the same unrelated category.

The key rule is:
RECOGNIZED ITEM -> normal TrailWeigh category.
UNKNOWN ITEM -> preserve safely; do not guess.
