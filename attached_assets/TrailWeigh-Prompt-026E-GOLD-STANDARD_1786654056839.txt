- the existing permanent Share URL must show that newly saved background on next reload/open.

- it must continue showing the prior last-saved background.

- Do not generate a new URL merely because the owner changes/saves background.

D. Reviewer sandbox edits remain isolated.
- A reviewer may change background locally inside Review.
- Those reviewer changes must not modify owner data.

E. When the owner's saved source changes and the existing Review architecture decides to
reseed because sourceVersion changed, the reseeded Review state must start from the owner's
newly saved background.
