One surgical correction to R0080P2, zero other changes:

**Right-chevron label fixed** — every `ChevronBox` with `direction="right"` was hardcoded to render the visible text label `'More'`. Changed to `'Next'`. The aria-label (`"Next controls"`) was always correct; only the visible span text was wrong.

## B. Defect Corrected from R0080P2

### Defect — Right-chevron visible text was `'More'` in all four groups

**Root cause (line 848):**
```typescript
// R0080P2 (wrong):
{direction === 'left' ? 'Back' : 'More'}

// R0080P3 (correct):
{direction === 'left' ? 'Back' : 'Next'}
```

**Why the tests did not catch it:** The R0080P2 tests relied on `aria-label="Next controls"` for all chevron lookups. The aria-label was correct; only the visible rendered text was wrong. No test asserted the text content of the `<span>` inside the button.

**Impact by group (before fix):**

| Group | Visible labels (R0080P2) | Problem |
|-------|--------------------------|---------|
| Group 1 | Locker \| Summary \| Add \| Search \| **More →** | Right chevron said "More" |
| Group 2 | Back \| Undo \| Redo \| Reset \| **More →** | Right chevron said "More" |
| Group 3 | Back \| Camera \| Photos \| Preview \| **More →** | Right chevron said "More" |
| Group 4 | Back \| Save \| Share \| **More ⋯** \| **More →** | Two controls both labeled "More" |

**After fix (R0080P3):**

| Group | Visible labels |
|-------|----------------|
| Group 1 | Locker \| Summary \| Add \| Search \| **Next →** |
| Group 2 | Back \| Undo \| Redo \| Reset \| **Next →** |
| Group 3 | Back \| Camera \| Photos \| Preview \| **Next →** |
| Group 4 | Back \| Save \| Share \| **More ⋯** \| **Next →** |

## F. Design Notes

- **Label intent:** "Next" communicates forward progression between control groups. "More" was always the wrong label — it implies an overflow menu (like a hamburger or ellipsis). The aria-label was correct from the start; only the visible text span was wrong.
- **Group 4 disambiguation:** Group 4 now has two clearly distinct controls near the right end: `More ⋯` (the `MoreHorizontal` icon NavBox, opens settings/tools) and `Next →` (the `ChevronRight` ChevronBox, wraps to Group 1). Previously both said "More", making the navigation intent invisible.
- **No behaviour change:** The fix is purely cosmetic — the `onClick` handlers, `aria-label` attributes, swipe logic, and wrap/clamp behaviour are unchanged.
