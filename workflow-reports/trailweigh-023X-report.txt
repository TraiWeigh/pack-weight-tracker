2. ROOT CAUSE OF INCORRECT PLACEMENT
--------------------------------------
The right toolbar flex container used `flex flex-wrap justify-between`. With the
365 px desktop column (minus lg:pl-3 = 12 px and lg:pr-9 = 36 px = 317 px
effective width), the sidebar Open/Close pill (~116 px) + BgEdit+Share group
(~208 px) + gap-2 (8 px) summed to ~332 px — exceeding the 317 px container.
`flex-wrap` caused the overflow item (BgEdit+Share group) to move to a second
row, placing the sidebar Open/Close alone on the first row above Background Edit.


3. EXACT LAYOUT/CSS CHANGE
---------------------------

Change A — Right toolbar container:

  BEFORE:
    className="order-first lg:order-last relative flex flex-wrap justify-between
               gap-2 pb-3 lg:pl-3 lg:pr-9 flex-shrink-0"

  AFTER:
    className="order-first lg:order-last relative flex items-center gap-2 pb-3
               lg:pl-3 lg:pr-2 flex-shrink-0"

  What changed:
    - Removed `flex-wrap`     → prevents items wrapping to a second row
    - Removed `justify-between` → replaced by ml-auto on the BgEdit+Share group
    - Added `items-center`    → vertically aligns sidebar OC and BgEdit+Share
    - Changed `lg:pr-9` (36 px) → `lg:pr-2` (8 px)
      Reason: lg:pr-9 was sized for the old lg:justify-end layout. With ml-auto
      used instead, the large right padding is no longer needed and was consuming
      17 px of space that caused the overflow.

  With lg:pr-2: effective desktop width = 365 - 12 (pl-3) - 8 (pr-2) = 345 px.
  Items total = ~332 px. Fits with ~13 px to spare.

Change B — BgEdit+Share group wrapper:

  BEFORE:
    <div className="flex items-center gap-2">

  AFTER:
    <div className="flex items-center gap-2 ml-auto">

  What changed:
    - Added `ml-auto`  → pushes BgEdit+Share group to the right edge, keeping
      the sidebar Open/Close flush against the left of the right toolbar column.

Net result:
  [ Open | Close ]                    [ Background Edit ] [ Share ]
  ← left-aligned (ml-0)              ← right-aligned via ml-auto →
