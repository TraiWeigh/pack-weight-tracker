## What changed

### 1 — Tap anywhere on the category bar opens or closes that category

The outer category-header div now carries `onClick={() => handleCatToggle(catName)}` and `cursor: pointer`. A tap on any part of the bar — the coloured wedge area, the category name, the item count, the weight display, or any blank space — toggles the category accordion.

Gesture disambiguation is fully preserved:
- **Long-press reorder** — the `onClickCapture` on the same div calls `e.stopPropagation() + preventDefault()` when `reorderJustHappenedRef.current` is true, so the trailing click after a reorder never reaches `onClick`.
- **Horizontal swipe (reveal actions)** — `SwipeDeleteRow`'s `onClickCapture` on its content div swallows the trailing click when `justDraggedRef.current` is true (after a drag) or when it is closing an open reveal, so those gestures never reach the outer div's `onClick`.
- **Wedge button** — its `onClick` calls `e.stopPropagation()` before `handleCatToggle`, so the click is consumed by the wedge and the outer div does **not** fire a second toggle.

### 2 — Category name area no longer opens an options modal

The category name `<button>` (which previously opened a rename/delete options modal) is converted to a plain `<div>` with `data-testid="cat-name-<name>"`. Clicks on the name area now bubble up to the outer div's toggle handler. The category's delete/confirmation flow remains reachable via the swipe-reveal Delete button.

### 3 — Navigation drawer always slides in from the LEFT

`NavDrawer.tsx`: `fromLeft` was previously `handedness === 'right'`, meaning the drawer slid from the right in left-handed mode. Changed to `fromLeft = true` — the drawer always slides from the left regardless of the handedness setting. The handedness toggle inside the drawer still works; it no longer affects which side the panel appears on.
