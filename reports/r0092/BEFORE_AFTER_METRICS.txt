The visual-viewport bottom gap is computed live as:
max(0, window.innerHeight - (visualViewport.offsetTop + visualViewport.height)).
At normal Chromium dimensions it is 0px, preserving the existing visual spot. On a
Safari state where visualViewport is shorter or offset, the same measured gap moves the
bar, content reservation, and raised decks together to the usable viewport edge.
