ROOT CAUSES
1. Bottom Next controls used a centered icon/label directly inside the full-width button,
   leaving the visual content too close to the physical right edge even though the button
   hitbox was correct.
2. The logo group retained a 6px left padding after the 44px hamburger target, producing a
   visible gap that was unnecessary for the header's flex layout.
3. Summary metrics were in a vertical flex stack without right-edge alignment. The
   category line's visual text ended left of the Selected line even though the outer stack
   itself was inside the R0090 gutter.

SMALLEST COHERENT FIX
The patch is limited to three internal alignment changes:
- Remove the logo group's 6px left padding. Hamburger size, AppBar outer 8px frame,
  logo size, title size, and header height stay unchanged.
- Add `alignItems: flex-end` to the existing Summary right metrics stack. Both visible
  lines now share the same right edge; no new Summary chevron was introduced.
- Shift only the right-direction ChevronBox's inner icon/label content left by 8px using
  an internal transform. The Bottom Box Groups button, width, height, hitbox, and group
  navigation remain unchanged.
