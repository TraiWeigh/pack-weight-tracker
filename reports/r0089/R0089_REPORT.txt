STANDING QUESTIONS FOR FUTURE PROMPTS
1. What did this task teach us?
   In this UI, "right edge" was not one value: Summary, header grids, item details, and
   photo controls had different inherited padding. A single named right-gutter token is
   safer than isolated offsets, but Summary flex space must be checked so right-side
   movement does not create unintended list-name truncation.

2. Was anything unclear, unnecessary, risky, or better expressed another way?
   The revised prompt clearly established that this was a coordinated content inset, not
   a weight-only adjustment. The explicit exclusions for shell width, wedges, AppBar,
   Bottom Box Groups, and Safari behavior prevented scope creep. The only risk was
   Summary text-width loss, which was corrected before delivery.

3. What Replit/project detail helps future work?
   Use target-package typechecks and isolated Playwright outputs for TrailWeigh work;
   wider workflows have unrelated legacy failures. Chromium can verify right-edge
   geometry, but real iPhone Safari remains the authority for final visual approval.
